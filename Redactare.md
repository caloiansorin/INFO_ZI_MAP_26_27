# EasyBudget

Aplicatie de gestionare a bugetului.

## Abstract

As young adults gain more independence, many of them struggle with many things. One of the more important problems is managing their budget, as modern youth tends to use __mental budgeting tactics__ [1] rather than old methods such as keeping a journal or spreadsheet, since it is too tedious. This has lead to the increase of financial illiteracy, as data confirms that only 34% of all adults possess the minimum financial literacy [2] with this number being as low 22% among young adukts [3]. This specific problem is what **EasyBudget** is trying to solve. It is an easy-to-use budget app with many useful features such as a __self-adjustable daily budget__, spending history, easy categorization, end-of-month summaries, monthly payments tracking, and more. The app is made in __Python__ with __FastAPI__ for its backend __Flet__ for it's frontend, which allows for high maintainability and decent performance.
